$1,000 now or $1,500 next year? Notice which one your gut wanted first.

Your brain discounts the future. Rewards feel smaller the further away they are (psychologists call it present bias). It shows up when you buy instead of save, close a trade early for a small win, or skip the workout.

Future you feels like a stranger. Make the future feel closer: name the goal, automate the wait, picture a specific day.
