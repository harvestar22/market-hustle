He guessed he spent $200 a year on coffee. He checked: closer to $1,200.

$4.60, five times a week, 52 weeks ≈ $1,196. Each cup felt like nothing.

Leaks, not floods, sink a lot of budgets. Big purchases get a second thought. Small ones never do, which is why they hide.

The twist: he didn't quit coffee. He decided on it. Three café days a week, home coffee the rest. He still enjoys it and spends a lot less.

Try this: track one category for 30 days. Coffee, delivery, subscriptions, games. Just one. Then choose, don't guess.

What's your small leak? Comment it, then track it for 30 days.
