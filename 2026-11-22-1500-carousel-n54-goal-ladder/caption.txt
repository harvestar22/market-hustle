Turn a big goal into this week's task in 4 steps.

1. Year: become a consistent trader. Good for direction, useless for Tuesday.
2. Quarter: follow my plan for 60 trading days. Now it's measurable.
3. Month: journal every session. One habit that makes the quarter likely.
4. Week: 5 journal entries. Small enough to count on one hand.

Only the bottom rung needs you today. Stop staring at the top of the ladder. Today's entry is the whole job.

Comment your year goal, then write down this week's rung. Save this ladder.
