The day you think you've figured it out, you stop learning.

Certainty feels like strength. "I already know this" is comfortable, and it also closes the door on anything new.

Curiosity keeps the door open. Markets change. People change. You change. Curious people adjust before they're forced to.

One question helps: what am I missing? Ask it before a trade, a purchase, an argument. It's the cheapest risk check there is.

And keep a beginner's notebook. One line a day: something you didn't know yesterday.

Stay a student. Not because you're behind, but because there's always a next level.
