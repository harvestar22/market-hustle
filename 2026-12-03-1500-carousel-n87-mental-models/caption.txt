3 mental models for better decisions.

01 Inversion: ask what would make this fail. List the ways it goes wrong, then avoid them. Often easier than chasing perfect.

02 Second-order thinking: and then what? The first effect is obvious. The second and third are where the surprises live.

03 Opportunity cost: what am I giving up? Every yes is a no to something else. Your time and money only go one place at a time.

Use one before every big choice. Screenshot the card. Thirty seconds of thinking can save months of regret.

Which one do you use most? Comment 1, 2 or 3, and save this for your next big decision.
