Some days you'll do it tired.

Not inspired, not excited. Just tired. Bad sleep, long day, nothing left, and the routine is still waiting.

Those days say the most about who you're becoming. Anyone shows up when it feels good.

So lower the bar and keep the streak. One journal line instead of a page. Review one trade instead of the whole week. Tired reps are still reps. And on tired days, the best trade is often no trade at all. Following that rule is a rep too.

What's your tired-day minimum? Comment it, and save this for tonight.
