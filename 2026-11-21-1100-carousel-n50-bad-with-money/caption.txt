He said "I'm bad with money." So he was.

Every impulse buy proved the story true: "See? I'm bad with money. Why bother tracking?" Repeat.

It's easier to act like the person you think you are. Goals sit on top. The story you tell about yourself sits underneath, steering.

So change the sentence. Not "I'm great with money," just something true you can start living today: "I'm someone who tracks money." Then prove it with one tiny action a day, like writing down today's spending. Small proof changes the story.

What story do you tell about yourself? Comment your new sentence.
