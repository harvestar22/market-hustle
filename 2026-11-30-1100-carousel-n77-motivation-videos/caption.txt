He watched motivation videos for an hour. Then did nothing.

He felt productive without producing. The feeling of progress showed up. The progress didn't.

Inspiration is a starter, not fuel. A match can light the engine. It can't drive the car for a month.

The flip: often, action comes first and motivation follows. Five minutes of doing tends to create more drive than an hour of watching.

The swap: replace one video with one action. Instead of "10 habits of rich traders," write tomorrow's plan. Same 10 minutes.

Watch less. Do the boring rep. Nobody films the rep that matters. It still counts.

What will you do instead of the next video? Yes, including this one. Comment your action, then close the app.
