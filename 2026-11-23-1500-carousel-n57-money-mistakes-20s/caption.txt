4 money mistakes that feel normal in your 20s.

1. Financing things you'll stop using. The excitement fades in weeks. The payments can last years.
2. No buffer for surprises. One repair bill goes on a card, and a small surprise becomes a long debt.
3. Lifestyle rising with every raise. Earn more, spend more, save the same.
4. Learning to invest from hype accounts. Screenshots of wins aren't a strategy. Learn from sources that show the risks too.

Don't try to fix all four. Fix one this month.
