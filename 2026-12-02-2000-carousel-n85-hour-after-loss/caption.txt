The hour after a loss defines you.

Most people react. The sting is fresh and every instinct says do something, right now. That's when the three classic mistakes happen: revenge trading, doubling size, or quitting altogether. Different moves, same root: a decision made while upset.

Disciplined traders go quiet. No new trades, no hot takes. They let the adrenaline pass first.

The protocol:
Walk. Leave the screen. Get outside.
Write. What happened? Did you follow your plan?
Wait. No new trade until the urge is gone.

Then return with a clear head. Same rules, same size. The loss is data, not a debt to win back.

Save this for the next red day. What do you do after a loss?
