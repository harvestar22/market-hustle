He wouldn't sell below what he paid. The price didn't care.

He bought at $40, so in his head $40 was what it was "really" worth. But the market doesn't know what you paid. Your entry is on your screen, not anyone else's.

That's anchoring: the first number you see sticks and quietly becomes the "right" price, even when nothing supports it.

The better question isn't "what did I pay?" It's "would I buy it here?" If the answer is no, your entry price isn't a reason to stay. Decide your stop and your reason to exit before you buy, so "break even" never becomes the plan.
