Patience is underrated in money.

Compounding needs time more than talent. The curve looks flat for a long time, then it doesn't. Most of the growth sits at the end.

The problem: it's easy to interrupt. Selling early. Spending early. Quitting early. Different scissors, same result. Each cut sends you back to the flat part, right before it was about to bend.

So leave good things alone longer. Savings, skills, habits, friendships. Stop digging them up to check if they're growing.

Let time do its work. Your job is to keep showing up and stay out of the way.
