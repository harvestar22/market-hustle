His parents fought about money. Now he avoids checking his balance.

Money habits often start before your first paycheck. Raised voices at the kitchen table, bills hidden in a drawer. Kids notice more than adults think.

Avoidance feels like peace. It's just delay. The number doesn't change because you don't look. It just gets a head start.

The shift: look gently, not harshly. You're not grading yourself. You're reading a map so you know where you are.

The practice: once a week, open the app for two minutes. Same day, same time, with a coffee. Look, note one thing, close it.

Familiar numbers stop being scary. You can't fix what you won't look at.
