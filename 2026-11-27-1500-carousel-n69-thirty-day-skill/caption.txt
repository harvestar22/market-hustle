How to start any skill in 30 days: a simple 4-week plan.

Week 1: basics only. Find the 3 to 5 fundamentals everyone agrees on and ignore the advanced stuff.

Week 2: practise daily, badly. Short sessions, every day. For trading, use paper trades or a demo, not real money.

Week 3: get feedback. Record yourself, review your journal, and find the one mistake you repeat.

Week 4: teach it to someone. Explaining shows what you truly understand, and what you don't yet.

Be honest: 30 days won't make you an expert. It makes you started. And started is the hardest part.
