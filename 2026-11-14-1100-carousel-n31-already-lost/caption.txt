He'd already lost $400. So he kept the trade open. "If I close now, it was all for nothing." The $400 became $900.

The loss already happened. Closing doesn't create it. That's the sunk cost trap: what you've already put in makes you keep going, even when the future looks worse.

Ask one clean question: if I had no position right now, would I enter here? If the answer is no, the exit is your answer.

Ever held a loser because you'd already lost? Comment "guilty."

Illustrative story. Education only, not financial advice. Trading involves risk of loss.
