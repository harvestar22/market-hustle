Stack new habits onto old ones. Here's the formula.

"After I [current habit], I will [new habit]."

The old habit becomes the trigger, so you don't have to remember. A few examples:
· After coffee, write today's plan.
· After lunch, take a 10-minute walk.
· After closing the laptop, journal one line.

Keep each new habit under 2 minutes at first. Make it easy to start, then grow it once it's automatic.
