Manage energy, not just time. A simple map.

Step one: map your day into high, medium and low. Watch yourself for a week. When are you sharp? When do you fade?

High energy: your hardest thinking work. Study, planning, analysis, writing. Protect these hours like appointments.

Medium energy: meetings, calls, conversations. Other people bring some energy with them.

Low energy: admin, email, chores. Things that need doing but not much thinking.

The one rule: no trades or big decisions in the low zone. If it can cost you money or a relationship, it waits for a higher-energy hour.
