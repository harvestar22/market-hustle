Know your "enough" before the market tells you.

Without a number, more is never enough. A good day becomes "could've been better" and the cup never feels full.

Greed moves the finish line. You hit the target, then raise it. You hold a winner too long, then give it back.

So write down what enough looks like: per trade, per week, per year. In writing, before you start.

Then protect it. Hit your number? Stop for the day. Done is a decision, not a feeling.

Enough is freedom. Knowing when to stop gives you your time, your calm and your evenings back.

What's your enough? Save this and write your number tonight.
