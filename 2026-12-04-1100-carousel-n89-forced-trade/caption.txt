He missed the move. So he forced the next one.

Missing out felt like losing money. He kept counting what he "would have made", even though his account hadn't changed at all.

So he entered a setup that wasn't there. No level, no signal. Just a need to be in something, anything.

Missed trades cost $0. Forced trades cost real money.

Another real setup will come. Your capital is what lets you take it.

The rule: missed a move? Step away for 15 minutes. Come back and only take setups from your plan, not the one you wish you'd caught.
