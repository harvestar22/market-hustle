He blamed the market. The market didn't notice.

Blame feels good for a minute. "It was rigged." "The news did it." "Bad luck." The sting fades, and nothing changes.

Blame hands your power to something you can't control. If the market caused it, the market has to fix it. It won't.

Ownership asks a different question: what part was mine? The size? The timing? The skipped stop? Not to punish yourself, but to find the lever.

Even 10% ownership gives you something to fix. You don't control the 90%. You fully control the 10%.

Fix one thing per week. One rule tightened, one habit changed.

What are you blaming right now? Comment "mine" if you're owning it.
