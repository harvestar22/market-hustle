He had the idea for two years. Someone else launched it.

He was waiting for the perfect moment. More research. A better logo. After the holidays. The perfect moment never showed up.

Meanwhile, his friend launched a rough version in a weekend. A basic page, a simple offer. Ugly, but real. Then she started fixing it.

Ideas are cheap. Small launches teach you. You learn more from ten real customers than from ten months of planning.

Version one is supposed to be embarrassing. If v1 feels polished, you probably waited too long.

Launch small. Learn fast. Improve. Each lap makes it better. Waiting adds zero laps.

What idea are you sitting on? Comment it. Then start small this week.
