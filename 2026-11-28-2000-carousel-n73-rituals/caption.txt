Rituals beat motivation.

The formula: same time, same place, same first step. 7 AM, the same chair, coffee, then open the journal. Every time.

Why it works: rituals remove the decision to start. Motivation asks "do I feel like it?" A ritual skips the question.

Many athletes use pre-game routines: the same warm-up, the same steps, before every serve or kick. You can borrow the idea.

Build one 5-minute start ritual. Phone in another room. Water. Review yesterday's notes. Then begin.

Motivation visits. Rituals live here. On the days you feel nothing, the ritual still knows what to do next.
