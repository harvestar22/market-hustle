3 ways traders set stops, and what each one is really saying about your trade.

01 Structure: beyond the level that proves you wrong. Long off support? The stop goes a little below it. If price gets there, the idea is broken.

02 Volatility: based on how much price normally moves, often a multiple of average range (ATR). A wider stop means a smaller position size.

03 Time: exit if the idea hasn't worked within the window you set up front. Dead money ties up focus and capital.

None is perfect. Pick one and be consistent, so your results can actually be reviewed.

And never widen a stop mid-trade. Moving it further away doesn't make you right. It just makes the loss bigger.
