A letter from you, one year from now.

"Thank you for starting when it felt small."
"Thank you for the boring weeks."
"Thank you for the month you almost quit, and didn't."
"I'm not finished. But I'm proud."

Ten minutes a day looks like nothing. Boring weeks don't get applause. Everyone has a month where quitting feels reasonable. Future you is built in exactly those moments.

Write yours tonight. Three lines: what will future you thank you for? Then go earn it.

Save this and read it again in a year. What's one line in your letter? Drop it below.
