Play long games with long-term people.

Short games: quick wins, quick losses. Hot tips, fast flips, fair-weather friends. Every round starts from zero.

Long games: trust, skills, savings, health. Each round builds on the last. Nothing resets.

For years, long games look slow. A sapling looks the same on Monday as it did last Monday, and people lose interest.

Then one day they look unfair. "How did they get so lucky?" They didn't. They just didn't stop.

Choose your games, and the people you play them with. Long-term people keep their word when it's inconvenient.
