Not every season is for harvest.

Winter: learn. Study, backtest, journal. Nothing to show, everything to build.
Spring: plant small habits. Small size, simple rules, repeated daily.
Summer: work when it's hot. When conditions fit your plan, show up fully.
Autumn: collect, then review. Take stock, then rest before the cycle starts again.

Know your season, and don't judge it by someone else's. Their summer might be your winter. Both are part of the same year.

Which season are you in? Comment winter, spring, summer or autumn, and save this for the slow months.

Education only, not financial advice.
