Become boring in the right places.

Boring savings. Boring routine. Boring risk. Automatic transfers, the same wake-up time, the same position size.

Then an exciting life everywhere else: trips, projects, people, ideas. The colour goes where it's worth it.

Boring systems fund exciting choices. The trip happens because the savings were dull. The calm comes from the routine.

The mix-up: exciting money often means a boring life later. Thrill in your account can cost freedom somewhere else. Swap them.

Where do you need more boring? Comment one area, and save this for the next time boring feels like failing.
