He checked his open trade 40 times in an hour.

The price didn't move faster. His stress did. Every tick up felt like relief, every tick down felt like danger, and every check was another chance to break the plan. At 2:15 he closed early. Then it hit his target.

The plan was fine. Watching it was what broke it. Set alerts at your stop and target (or use bracket orders if your platform has them), then step away. The plan watches the trade. You don't have to.
