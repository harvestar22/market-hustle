A 10-minute morning setup for focused days. No cold plunge required.

Minutes 1–2: water, light, no phone. Drink a glass, open the curtains. Your feed can wait.

Minutes 3–5: write today's one priority. If only one thing gets done, which one makes the day a win?

Minutes 6–8: plan the first 90 minutes. Put the priority first and decide what "done" looks like.

Minutes 9–10: clear the desk. Only what the first task needs.

Then start. No more prep. Begin the priority before anything else.

Save this and try it tomorrow. Which step would change your mornings most?
