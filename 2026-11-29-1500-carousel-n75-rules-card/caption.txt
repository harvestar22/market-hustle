Write your trading rules on one card. Here's the template.

If they don't fit on a card, you won't follow them under pressure.

Line 1, markets I trade: fewer markets, deeper knowledge.
Line 2, the setup I take: describe it so a stranger could spot it. If you can't, it's a feeling, not a setup.
Line 3, max risk per trade: a number, decided in advance. Not "it depends."
Line 4, max trades per day: a cap stops one bad mood becoming ten trades.
Line 5, I stop for the day when: the most important line. Write it while you're calm.

Keep the card where you can see it, and read it before the first trade every day.
