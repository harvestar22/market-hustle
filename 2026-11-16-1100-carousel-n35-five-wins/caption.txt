Five wins in a row. Then he doubled his size.

The wins felt like proof he'd figured it out. But streaks show up in random games too: five heads in a row has about a 1 in 32 chance, and over enough flips you'll see it. On Monday, one normal loss at double size erased the whole week.

Size should follow your rules, not your mood. A simple guardrail: no size change within 24 hours of a streak, winning or losing. Decide size changes on a calm day, in writing.
