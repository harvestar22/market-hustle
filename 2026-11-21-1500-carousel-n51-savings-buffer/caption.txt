Why a boring savings buffer makes you a better trader.

Without a buffer, every loss feels like rent money. When your trading account is also your safety net, a normal losing trade feels like a threat, and desperate money makes desperate decisions: moving stops, sizing up to win it back, forcing trades that aren't there.

With a buffer, a loss is just a loss. Bills are covered either way, and that calm is what lets you follow the plan.

Step 1: start with one month of essentials, kept separate from your trading account.
Step 2: grow the buffer before you grow your position size.
