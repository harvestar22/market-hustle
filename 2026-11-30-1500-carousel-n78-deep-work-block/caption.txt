Set up a 90-minute deep work block in five steps.

1. Before you start: one clear task, written down. "Work on trading" is vague. "Backtest one setup on 20 charts" is a task.

2. Phone in another room. Not face down, not on silent. Another room. Distance beats willpower.

3. One tab, one window. Close email, chat and news. If it's not the task, it's not open.

4. After 90 minutes: walk, water, no screens. Scrolling isn't a break. It's more input.

5. Build up to two blocks a day. Over a five-day week, that's 15 hours of real focus.
