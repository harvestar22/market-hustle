The 5-line trading journal that actually gets used.

1. Setup: why I entered, in one sentence.
2. Plan: entry, stop and target, written before you click.
3. Feeling: one word, before entry. Calm? Bored? Revenge?
4. Result: did I follow the plan? Yes or no. Grade the decision, not the outcome.
5. Lesson: one sentence.

Short enough to write after every trade. That's the whole trick. Over a few weeks, line 3 shows patterns no chart will.
