"I knew it was going to drop." No, he didn't.

After the move, it always looks obvious. Once you know the ending, every candle before it seems to point there. That's hindsight bias: your memory quietly edits what you actually thought. On Monday he wasn't sure. By Friday he "always knew."

The cost is the fake confidence that leaks into the next trade: "I called the last one," so he sizes up on a feeling he never had.

The fix: write your prediction before, and check it after. What you expect, why, and how sure you are. Date it and don't edit it. After a few weeks you'll know your real hit rate.
