Risk isn't the enemy. Unmeasured risk is.

Everything worth doing has risk: a new job, a business, a relationship, a trade. Zero risk usually means zero change.

The real question is how much, and why. What can you lose, what can you gain, and is the trade worth it to you?

Measure it before, not after. Before, it's a plan. After, it's an excuse.

Small, repeatable risks can build big results, one manageable step at a time, where no single step can knock you off the staircase.

Reckless and careful people both take risks. Only one of them measures.

What risk are you measuring this week? Comment it, and save this for your next big decision.
