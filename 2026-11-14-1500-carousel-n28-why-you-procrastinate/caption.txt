6 pictures that explain why you procrastinate.

Most procrastination isn't about time. It's about the feeling the task gives you: boredom, uncertainty, or fear of doing it badly. The dread is usually worst right before you start, and it fades once you're moving.

The fix is boring on purpose: shrink the task until starting feels almost silly. Open the file. Write one line. Set a 5-minute timer.

What are you avoiding right now? Comment the task, then give it 5 minutes today.
