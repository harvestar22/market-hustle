Everyone was talking about it. So he bought the top.

By the time something is on every feed, the move has often already happened. FOMO feels like opportunity, but underneath it's the fear of being left behind. He wasn't following a plan. He was following a crowd: no entry rule, no stop, no idea when he'd be wrong.

Missing a move costs $0. Chasing one can cost a lot. A simple rule: if you found it on social media, wait 24 hours. If it still fits your plan tomorrow, you've lost nothing.
