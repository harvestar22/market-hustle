Big decision? Run these 4 tests first.

1. 10/10/10: how will I feel about this in 10 minutes, 10 months and 10 years?
2. Regret: looking back a year from now, which choice would I regret not making?
3. Reversibility: can I undo it cheaply? Easy to reverse, decide fast. Hard to reverse, slow down.
4. One night: if it's big and "urgent," sleep on it. Pressure to decide right now is a warning sign.

Then decide and commit. Re-deciding every day costs more than a decent choice.
