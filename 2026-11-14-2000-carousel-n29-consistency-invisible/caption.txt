Why consistency feels invisible before it compounds.

Day 1 and day 30 often look the same from the outside. Think of an ice cube warming from 25°F to 31°F: nothing seems to happen. At 32°F it starts to melt. Every degree counted, but only the last one showed.

That's why it helps to track inputs, not results. Results lag. Your reps don't: plan followed, journal written, rules kept.
