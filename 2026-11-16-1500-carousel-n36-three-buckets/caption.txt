Split every paycheck into 3 buckets. That's the whole budget.

1. Needs: rent, food, bills, transport, minimum debt payments.
2. Future: emergency fund, savings, investing, extra debt payoff.
3. Life: food out, trips, hobbies, gifts. Spend this one guilt-free.

The trick is the order. Set an automatic transfer to Future on payday, before the spending starts. If you wait to save what's left, there's often nothing left.

There's no perfect percentage. Adjust the sizes to your situation, but keep the order and keep Future above zero.
