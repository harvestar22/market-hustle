The chart is a mirror.

Impatient eyes see entries everywhere. Fearful eyes see crashes everywhere. Greedy eyes see rockets everywhere.

Same candles, three different stories. The chart didn't change. The person looking did. Before it shows you the market, it shows you yourself.

So work on the person looking: sleep, rules, a written plan, a calm head. Clearer eyes see a clearer chart.

What do you usually see: entries, crashes or rockets? Comment one, and save this for your next session.
