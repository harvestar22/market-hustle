He quit guitar at week 3. He quit trading at month 2.

Different skills, same exit point.

Every skill has an ugly middle. The start is exciting and the end is rewarding, but the middle is where people leave. Progress comes fast early, then there's a long flat stretch where it feels like nothing is happening.

That flat part is where skill is forming. Nothing shows above ground yet. Underneath, the roots are spreading.

When he finally pushed through one, it clicked. Not overnight, but the song he couldn't play in month one, he played in month four.

The trick: name the plateau when you reach it. "This is the flat part. It's supposed to feel like this."
