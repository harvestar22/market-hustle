Discipline is a form of self-respect.

Every promise you keep to yourself is a brick. Said you'd journal, and did. Said you'd stop at your limit, and did. Every broken one quietly teaches you not to believe yourself, until "I'll start Monday" stops meaning anything.

Start with tiny promises, small enough that you can't talk yourself out of them. Keep them stubbornly, even when you're tired, busy or bored. Self-trust compounds. One day the hard rule, the one that matters in a bad trade, is just something you do.
