Every bad trade he made happened after 11 PM.

Tired brains tend to take bigger risks. After a long day, "just one more" feels reasonable. So does doubling down. At night, discipline is at its weakest and charts look exciting. Quiet room, bright screen, nobody to check you.

So he set a rule: no new trades after 10. Not a feeling. A rule, written down, decided when he was rested.

His trading got boring, in a good way. Fewer impulse trades, fewer 2 AM regrets, mornings without damage control.

Decision quality depends on your state, not just your skill.

What time do your worst decisions happen? Comment the hour, then set your cut-off.
