Two ways to pay off debt. Pick the one you'll stick to.

Snowball: pay minimums on everything, then throw every extra dollar at the smallest balance. When it's gone, roll that payment into the next one. Quick wins keep you going.

Avalanche: same minimums, but the extra goes to the highest interest rate first. Mathematically, you pay less interest overall.

Snowball is for motivation. Avalanche is for math. The best method is the one you don't quit.

Either way: automate the minimums, attack one.

Snowball or avalanche? Comment your pick.
