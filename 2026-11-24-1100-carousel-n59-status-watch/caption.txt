He bought the watch to feel successful. The feeling lasted about a week.

By day ten it was just a watch. The payments were still there.

Status buys attention, not security. Others notice for a moment, and the payments last months. Most people are too busy thinking about themselves to keep looking at your wrist.

Real status is having options: leaving a bad job, saying no to a bad deal, handling a surprise bill. Nobody sees it. You feel it.

Before the next big purchase, ask: would I buy this if nobody could see it? If yes, enjoy it. If no, you're paying for an audience.
