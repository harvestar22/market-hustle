Every serious decision is made in a quiet room.

Not in the group chat, where there are twelve opinions and nobody is responsible for your account. Not in the comments, where loud certainty passes for being right. Not at 2 AM after three losses.

Find your quiet room: a desk, a notebook, your rules, and no screen shouting at you. Even 15 minutes counts. Write the plan when you're calm. Follow it when it's loud.
