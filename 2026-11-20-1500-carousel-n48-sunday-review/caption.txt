The Sunday 15-minute review that runs your week.

Minutes 0–3: What went well? Name it so you can repeat it.
Minutes 3–7: What went badly? Pick one fix, not ten.
Minutes 7–11: What's the one priority this week?
Minutes 11–15: What could derail it? Late nights, a busy Wednesday, a revenge trade. Plan around it now.

Then put the priority on the calendar before you close the notebook. A priority without a time slot is just a wish.

Which question is hardest for you to answer honestly? Comment 1, 2, 3 or 4, and save this for Sunday.
