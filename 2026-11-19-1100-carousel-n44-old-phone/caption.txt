He kept the old phone for two more years.

His friends upgraded. He kept the cracked one and moved the payment he didn't have into savings every month. In year 1, nobody noticed. By year 3 he had a buffer and they had payments. When his car broke down, it was an annoying week, not a crisis.

Patience is choosing which version of you gets paid. Today-you wants the upgrade. Future-you wants options.

Try one "not yet" purchase this month. Not never, just not yet. Move what it would have cost into savings and see how it feels.
