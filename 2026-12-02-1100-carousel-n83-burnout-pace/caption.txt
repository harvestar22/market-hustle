He worked 80-hour weeks. Then nothing for a month.

Intensity without recovery borrows from next month. Late nights feel productive, but the bill shows up later as fog, mistakes and zero motivation.

Burnout isn't a badge. It's a gap in the chart. Nobody sees the weeks you couldn't show up, but they count.

Sustainable pace beats heroic bursts. Steady work keeps going. Bursts tend to stop.

So plan rest like you plan work. Put it in the calendar. If it isn't scheduled, it gets skipped.

Ambition is a marathon disguised as a sprint.
