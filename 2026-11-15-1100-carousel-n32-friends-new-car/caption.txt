His friend posted a new car. He felt poor for a week.

Nothing in his life had changed. Same rent, same savings, same car that runs fine. Only his reference point moved. He compared one highlight photo to his whole behind-the-scenes.

Comparison measures the wrong race. Different start, different goals, different finish line. The only full story you know is your own, so compare yourself to last month instead. Track three numbers that are only yours: savings, skill hours, sleep.
