Risk:reward in one picture.

Risk is what you lose if you're wrong: the distance from your entry to your stop. Reward is what you aim to gain if you're right: the distance from your entry to your target.

Why it matters: at 1:2, you can be right 1 in 3 times and break even. Two losses of 1, one win of 2. Net zero, before fees.

The catch: it only works if you honour the stop and the target. Move the stop and the 1 becomes 3. Grab profit early and the 2 becomes 0.5.

A ratio means nothing without discipline. Save this for your next plan, and comment "ratio" if you set yours before you enter.
