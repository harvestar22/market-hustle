In a room full of panic, the calm person decides.

Calm isn't feeling nothing. Calm traders still feel the drop in their stomach. Calm is feeling it and not obeying it: the feeling gets a vote, not the final say.

It's trained in small moments. Waiting for the candle to close. Not replying to the angry comment. Then, when it's loud: breathe, wait, then act on the plan.

Be the calm one. Not because you don't care, but because you've practised what to do when everyone else forgets.
