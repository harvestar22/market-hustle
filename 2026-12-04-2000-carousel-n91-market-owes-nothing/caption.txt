The market doesn't owe you anything.

Not for your effort. Twelve hours of screen time doesn't earn a winning trade. Price doesn't know you tried.

Not for your losses. Five red days don't mean a green one is "due". Each trade starts from zero.

What it tends to reward is preparation, patience and risk control. None of these guarantee results. They give you a fair chance to stay in the game.

Over time. Not on demand. It isn't a vending machine.

So show up prepared. Plan written. Risk set. Mind clear. Then let the market do what it does.

Save this and read it after a red day.

Education only, not financial advice. Trading involves risk of loss.
